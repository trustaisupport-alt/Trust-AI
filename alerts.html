Logistics & Supply

Package Clearance Traps

MEDIUM RISK

Fake delivery notifications asserting that a package is held in customs pending immediate clearance or delivery fees.

Warning Indicators:
  • SMS alerts containing suspicious or shortened links.
  • Demands for custom tax payments via third-party gateways.
  • Urgent countdown deadlines before package disposal.
Action Protocol: Open the official logistics app or browser domain directly to verify tracking IDs manually.

Recruitment & Career

Advance-Fee Employment

MEDIUM RISK

Fraudulent HR representatives promising high remote salaries while requiring upfront equipment, training, or activation deposits.

Warning Indicators:
  • High income promised for basic or minimal tasks.
  • Mandatory processing or registration fees prior to onboarding.
  • Interviews conducted strictly over messaging channels.
Action Protocol: Verify open requisitions directly on verified enterprise career portals.

Identity & Credentials

MFA & OTP Harvesting

CRITICAL THREAT

Social engineering vectors targeting One-Time Passwords (OTPs) and account recovery codes to bypass multi-factor authentication.

Warning Indicators:
  • Requests to forward SMS verification codes.
  • Caller claiming to be bank security rectifying an "account lock".
Action Protocol: Never disclose security PINs or OTPs. Financial institutions will never ask for access credentials.

Finance & Crypto

High-Yield Ponzi Schemes

HIGH RISK

Unlicensed trading platforms promising zero-risk capital growth, automated arbitrage returns, or guaranteed daily yields.

Warning Indicators:
  • Claims of zero risk paired with fixed return guarantees.
  • Withdrawal restrictions contingent on depositing additional capital.
Action Protocol: Verify regulatory licensing with official national financial conduct authorities.

Social Engineering

Emergency Impersonation

HIGH RISK

Bad actors impersonating close relatives, colleagues, or authorities requesting immediate emergency funds under distress.

Warning Indicators:
  • "This is my new number, please save it..." messages.
  • Demands for secrecy or immediate wire transfers.
Action Protocol: Call the contact directly using their previously verified phone number.

Infrastructure

Domain Spoofing & Phishing

HIGH RISK

Cloned portals and spoofed login interfaces crafted to harvest user credentials and payment card data.

Warning Indicators:
  • Misspelled domain names (e.g., `paypal-security-login.com`).
  • Shortened URLs concealing target site destinations.
Action Protocol: Inspect root domain extensions carefully and avoid clicking unverified links.
