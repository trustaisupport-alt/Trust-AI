Scam Alerts

Learn about common scams, recognize warning signs and know what to do before you send money, share information or click a suspicious link.

⚠️

Stay alert

Scammers often create urgency, promise unrealistic rewards or pretend to be someone you trust. Take a moment to verify unexpected requests before taking action.

Common Scam Alerts

🛍️
Online Shopping

Fake Online Stores

Fake stores may advertise expensive products at unusually low prices and ask you to pay before delivery.

Warning signs
  • Extremely low prices
  • Pressure to pay immediately
  • No reliable contact information
  • Requests for unusual payment methods
Stay safe: Research the seller and verify the website before making a payment.
📦
Delivery

Fake Package Messages

A message may claim that your package is waiting for a fee, customs payment or address confirmation.

Warning signs
  • Unexpected delivery messages
  • Suspicious links
  • Requests for payment
  • Urgent deadlines
Stay safe: Check your package directly through the official delivery company's website or app.
💼
Jobs & Employment

Fake Job Offers

Scammers may pretend to be recruiters and offer attractive jobs while asking applicants for money.

Warning signs
  • Unrealistic salary promises
  • Registration fees
  • Payment before employment
  • Pressure to act quickly
Stay safe: Verify the vacancy on the company's official website.
🔐
Account Security

OTP & Verification Code Scams

Someone may pretend to be a bank, company or support agent and ask for your OTP or verification code.

Warning signs
  • Someone asks for your OTP
  • Claims that your account is locked
  • Requests to forward a code
  • Creates fear or urgency
Stay safe: Never share your OTP, PIN or verification code with another person.
📈
Investment

Guaranteed Profit Scams

Fake investment opportunities may promise guaranteed profits, extremely high returns or zero risk.

Warning signs
  • Guaranteed returns
  • “No risk” investment claims
  • Pressure to deposit more money
  • Problems withdrawing funds
Stay safe: Research the company independently before investing.
👤
Impersonation

Fake Friends & Family

A scammer may pretend to be someone you know and claim they urgently need money or assistance.

Warning signs
  • “This is my new number”
  • Emergency money requests
  • Requests for secrecy
  • Unusual payment requests
Stay safe: Contact the person using a phone number you already know.
🌐
Websites & Links

Phishing Websites

Fake websites can imitate banks, social networks, shopping platforms and other trusted services.

Warning signs
  • Strange or misspelled domains
  • Unexpected login requests
  • Suspicious shortened links
  • Requests for passwords or codes
Stay safe: Type the official website address yourself instead of following an unexpected link.
📱
Social Media

Fake Profiles & Giveaways

Fake accounts may impersonate celebrities, businesses or popular pages and use giveaways to attract victims.

Warning signs
  • Requests for payment to claim a prize
  • New or suspicious accounts
  • Requests for personal information
  • “You have won” messages you never entered
Stay safe: Verify the account and promotion through the organization's official channels.
