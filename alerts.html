Stay alert before you act

Scammers often create urgency, promise unrealistic rewards or pretend to be people you trust. Stop, verify and think before sending money or sharing sensitive information.

Online Shopping

Fake Online Stores

Fake stores may advertise expensive products at unusually low prices and ask you to pay before delivery.

Warning signs
  • Extremely low prices
  • Pressure to pay immediately
  • No reliable contact information
  • Unusual payment methods
Stay safe: Research the seller and verify the website before making a payment.
📦
Delivery

Fake Package Messages

A message may claim that your package is waiting for a fee, customs payment or address confirmation.

Warning signs
  • Unexpected delivery messages
  • Suspicious links
  • Requests for payment
  • Urgent deadlines
Stay safe: Check your package through the official delivery company's website or app.
💼
Jobs & Employment

Fake Job Offers

Scammers may pretend to be recruiters and offer attractive jobs while asking applicants for money.

Warning signs
  • Unrealistic salary promises
  • Registration fees
  • Payment before employment
  • Pressure to act quickly
Stay safe: Verify the vacancy on the company's official website.
