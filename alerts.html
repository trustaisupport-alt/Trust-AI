Common Scam Alerts

Know the warning signs

🛍️
HIGH RISK
Online Shopping

Fake Online Stores

Fake stores may advertise expensive products at unusually low prices and ask you to pay before delivery.

Warning signs
  • Extremely low prices
  • Pressure to pay immediately
  • No reliable contact information
  • Unusual payment methods
Stay safe: Research the seller and verify the website before making a payment.
📦
CAUTION
Delivery

Fake Package Messages

A message may claim that your package is waiting for a fee, customs payment or address confirmation.

Warning signs
  • Unexpected delivery messages
  • Suspicious links
  • Requests for payment
  • Urgent deadlines
Stay safe: Check your package directly through the official delivery company's website or app.
💼
HIGH RISK
Jobs & Employment

Fake Job Offers

Scammers may pretend to be recruiters and offer attractive jobs while asking applicants for money.

Warning signs
  • Unrealistic salary promises
  • Registration fees
  • Payment before employment
  • Pressure to act quickly
Stay safe: Verify the vacancy on the company's official website.
🔐
CRITICAL
Account Security

OTP & Verification Code Scams

Someone may pretend to be a bank, company or support agent and ask for your OTP or verification code.

Warning signs
  • Someone asks for your OTP
  • Claims your account is locked
  • Requests to forward a code
  • Creates fear or urgency
Stay safe: Never share your OTP, PIN or verification code with another person.
📈
HIGH RISK
Investment

Guaranteed Profit Scams

Fake investment opportunities may promise guaranteed profits, extremely high returns or zero risk.

Warning signs
  • Guaranteed returns
  • “No risk” investment claims
  • Pressure to deposit more money
  • Problems withdrawing funds
Stay safe: Research the company independently before investing.
