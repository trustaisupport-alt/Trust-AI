Logistics

Customs & Delivery Traps

Fraudulent notifications requiring immediate clearance fees for non-existent parcels.

Key Risk Indicators
  • Unsolicited SMS notifications with shortened URLs.
  • High-pressure clearance payment deadlines.
  • Non-standard payment gateways.
Mitigation Protocol: Access official logistics portals directly via verified domain names—never click embedded SMS links.
💼
Employment

Advance-Fee Recruitment

Fraudulent recruiters demanding upfront equipment, training, or application fees.

Key Risk Indicators
  • Disproportionate remuneration for low-skill tasks.
  • Mandatory processing fees prior to contract signing.
  • Exclusive communication over unverified messaging channels.
Mitigation Protocol: Cross-reference open requisitions directly on corporate career portals.
🔐
Critical Threat

Credential & OTP Harvesting

Social engineering techniques designed to bypass Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA).

Key Risk Indicators
  • Requests to forward temporary verification SMS codes.
  • Impersonation of bank security units claiming "account lockouts".
Mitigation Protocol: Never disclose OTPs or security PINs. Legitimate institutions will never request authentication codes.
📈
Finance

High-Yield Investment Schemes

Fraudulent crypto and forex platforms promising risk-free capital multiplication.

Key Risk Indicators
  • Guaranteed fixed profit margins on volatile assets.
  • Restricted withdrawal mechanics contingent on additional deposits.
Mitigation Protocol: Validate regulatory compliance with accredited national financial authorities before committing funds.
👤
Identity

Executive & Social Impersonation

Bad actors impersonating family members, superiors, or official entities in crisis scenarios.

Key Risk Indicators
  • Claims of lost devices paired with unverified transfer requests.
  • Coercion demanding total transaction secrecy.
Mitigation Protocol: Perform out-of-band verification by calling the party directly through an established contact vector.
🌐
Infrastructure

Domain Spoofing & Phishing

Spoofed web interfaces built to intercept credit card data and personal identifiers.

Key Risk Indicators
  • Typosquatting domains (e.g., `paypaI-security.com`).
  • Obfuscated links utilizing URL shorteners.
Mitigation Protocol: Inspect top-level domain extensions and manually type primary web addresses into browser address bars.
