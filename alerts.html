🚨 Common Scam Alerts

Stay ahead of scammers. Learn how to identify high-risk tactics, fake offers, and social engineering patterns.

⚠️

Important Notice

Scammers constantly change their stories across social media, WhatsApp, SMS, and email. Always stop, verify details independently, and never rush into sending money or sharing security codes.

📦

Delivery & Package Scams

Scammers claim a package is on hold until you pay custom or clearance fees.

Warning Signs:
  • Unexpected parcel delivery notifications.
  • High-pressure demands to pay immediately.
  • Unfamiliar custom clearance payment links.
What to do: Contact the official courier service directly through their main website—never via the link provided in the message.
💼

Fake Job Offers

Fake recruiters offer high-paying jobs but demand upfront registration or equipment fees.

Warning Signs:
  • Unrealistic pay for minimal or basic work.
  • Upfront payment required before starting.
  • Recruiter contacts you exclusively via informal chat apps.
What to do: Research the company independently on verified corporate platforms or job boards.
🔐

OTP & PIN Theft

Scammers attempt to trick you into revealing login codes or bank transaction PINs.

Warning Signs:
  • A friend or stranger asks for a code sent to your phone.
  • Claims that the code is needed to "help" or "verify" your identity.
What to do: NEVER share your OTP, PIN, or password with anyone under any circumstances.
💰

Crypto & Investment Scams

Fraudulent platforms promising guaranteed profits and zero risk.

Warning Signs:
  • "Guaranteed" returns or fast money claims.
  • Pressure to deposit funds immediately into unfamiliar platforms.
What to do: Remember: high return always equals high risk. Real investments never guarantee fixed profits.
👤

Impersonation Tactics

Someone pretends to be a friend, family member, or company rep in trouble.

Warning Signs:
  • "Hi, this is my new WhatsApp number..."
  • Urgent financial emergencies combined with requests for secrecy.
What to do: Call the person directly on their known regular phone number to confirm.
🔗

Phishing & Malicious Links

Fake links designed to steal account passwords and credit card details.

Warning Signs:
  • Shortened URLs (bit.ly, tinyurl) masking real web addresses.
  • Alerts claiming your account will be suspended within 24 hours.
What to do: Inspect domain names carefully. Type official bank or web addresses manually.
