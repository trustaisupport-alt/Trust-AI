🚨 Scam Alerts

Learn about common scam methods and the warning signs that can help you recognize suspicious activity.

⚠️ Important

Scammers can use different stories, platforms, and techniques to gain your trust. A scam may appear as a message, phone call, social-media account, website, job offer, investment opportunity, or delivery notice.

If something feels suspicious, stop and verify it before sending money or sharing personal information.

📦 Delivery & Package Scams

A scammer may claim that you have a package waiting and that you must pay a delivery, customs, clearance, or shipping fee before it can be released.

Warning signs:

  • Unexpected package notification
  • Pressure to pay immediately
  • Requests for customs or clearance fees
  • Suspicious payment links
  • Requests for personal or banking information

What to do: Contact the delivery company through its official website or contact information instead of using the details provided in the suspicious message.

💼 Fake Job Scams

Fake recruiters may offer attractive jobs and then ask applicants to pay registration, training, equipment, or processing fees.

Warning signs:

  • Unexpected job offer
  • Very high pay for little work
  • Payment required before employment
  • Pressure to act quickly
  • Recruiter uses suspicious contact details

What to do: Research the company independently and verify the job through its official website or trusted channels.

💳 Payment & Banking Scams

Scammers may pretend to be banks, payment services, businesses, or people you know and ask you to transfer money or provide account information.

Warning signs:

  • Unexpected payment request
  • Pressure to transfer money
  • Requests for banking information
  • Claims that your account will be closed
  • Suspicious payment instructions

What to do: Contact your bank or payment provider using its official contact information.

🔐 OTP, PIN & Password Scams

Your OTP, PIN, password, and verification codes are sensitive security information.

Warning signs:

  • Someone asks you to send an OTP
  • Someone asks for your PIN
  • Someone asks for your password
  • Someone asks for a verification code
  • Someone claims to need the code to help you

What to do: Never share security codes with another person. If you receive an unexpected code, check your account directly.

🎁 Prize & Lottery Scams

A scammer may tell you that you have won a prize, lottery, giveaway, or cash reward that you never entered.

Warning signs:

  • You are told you won unexpectedly
  • A fee is required to claim the prize
  • You are asked for banking information
  • You are pressured to claim it immediately

What to do: Do not pay a fee simply because someone says you have won something.

💰 Investment & Crypto Scams

Fake investment opportunities may promise guaranteed profits, unusually high returns, or very little risk.

Warning signs:

  • Guaranteed profits
  • Promises of very high returns
  • Claims that there is no risk
  • Pressure to invest immediately
  • Requests to deposit money into an unfamiliar platform

What to do: Research the investment independently and be cautious of anyone promising guaranteed returns.

👤 Impersonation Scams

Someone may pretend to be your friend, relative, colleague, company representative, celebrity, or another trusted person.

Warning signs:

  • "This is my new number"
  • Requests for urgent financial help
  • Requests to keep the conversation secret
  • Unexpected account or contact changes
  • Pressure to act without verification

What to do: Contact the person through a separate, trusted communication channel to confirm their identity.

🔗 Phishing & Suspicious Links

Phishing messages often contain links designed to trick people into visiting fake websites or giving away sensitive information.

Warning signs:

  • Unexpected links
  • Shortened URLs
  • Urgent account warnings
  • Requests to log in immediately
  • Unusual website addresses

What to do: Avoid clicking suspicious links. Open the official website or app yourself instead.

🛡️ General Safety Rules

  • Never share your OTP, PIN, or password.
  • Verify unexpected requests independently.
  • Do not let pressure make you act quickly.
  • Be careful with unexpected payment requests.
  • Check website addresses carefully.
  • Do not assume something is safe because it looks professional.
