🛡️ Security Center

Learn simple ways to protect your accounts, money, devices, and personal information online.

🔑 Password Security

Use strong and unique passwords for important accounts. Avoid using the same password everywhere.

  • Use long and difficult-to-guess passwords.
  • Do not share your password with other people.
  • Avoid using your name, birthday, or simple numbers.
  • Use a password manager when appropriate.
  • Change a password if you believe it has been exposed.

🔐 OTP & Verification Code Security

One-time passwords and verification codes are designed to protect your account.

  • Never give your OTP to another person.
  • Never send a verification code through chat.
  • Do not read a security code to someone on a call.
  • If you receive an unexpected code, check your account.

🔗 Suspicious Link Safety

Be careful with links received through unexpected messages, emails, social media, or other channels.

  • Check the website address carefully.
  • Be cautious of shortened or unfamiliar links.
  • Do not enter passwords into suspicious websites.
  • When possible, open the official website yourself.

💳 Payment & Banking Safety

Always verify payment requests before sending money.

  • Confirm who you are paying.
  • Be careful with urgent payment requests.
  • Do not send money because someone is pressuring you.
  • Verify unexpected requests through another channel.
  • Contact your bank using official contact information if something looks suspicious.

🔄 Account Recovery

If you lose access to an account, use the platform's official recovery process.

  • Use the official website or application.
  • Do not pay strangers who promise to recover your account.
  • Never give someone your recovery codes.
  • Secure your email account because it may be used for recovery.

📱 Device Security

Keep your phone or computer protected when using banking, messaging, and social-media services.

  • Keep your operating system updated.
  • Install applications from trusted sources.
  • Use a screen lock.
  • Review unfamiliar applications and permissions.
  • Do not install software from suspicious links.

🚨 What If I Have Been Scammed?

If you think you have been scammed, act quickly and avoid sending additional money.

  • Stop communicating with the suspected scammer when appropriate.
  • Contact your bank or payment provider if money was involved.
  • Change compromised passwords.
  • Secure affected accounts.
  • Keep screenshots, messages, receipts, and transaction records.
  • Report the incident through appropriate official channels.

⚠️ Quick Security Rules

  • Never share your OTP.
  • Never share your PIN.
  • Never share your password.
  • Verify unexpected money requests.
  • Be careful with suspicious links.
  • Do not allow pressure to make your decisions for you.
