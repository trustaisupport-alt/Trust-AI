Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters to TrustAI. This page explains how information may be handled when you use our services.

TrustAI may collect information that is necessary to provide and improve its services. Depending on the features you use, this may include information you voluntarily provide and basic technical information about your use of the service.

If TrustAI provides account features, information associated with your account may be used to provide account-related services, maintain security and manage your preferences.

Some TrustAI preferences or activity may be stored locally on your device using browser storage. This may include settings such as notification preferences.

TrustAI should not sell your personal information. Information may only be shared when necessary to operate the service, protect users, comply with applicable law, or with your permission.

TrustAI uses reasonable security measures intended to protect information from unauthorized access, alteration or misuse. However, no internet service can guarantee complete security.

8. Your Choices

You may be able to manage certain preferences through TrustAI's Settings & Privacy page. You can also control browser storage through your browser settings.

9. Children's Privacy

TrustAI is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable laws.

10. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as TrustAI develops. When important changes are made, the updated version will be published on this page.

11. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact TrustAI through the official Contact TrustAI page.
